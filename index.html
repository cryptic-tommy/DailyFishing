A little guide to the pond

How to play

Catch as much as you can in five casts. Follow one step at a time.

1

Choose your tackle

Read today's note. Choose your rod, bait, and sinker. Small fish move fast; big fish pull hard. Willow steers faster. Oak reduces fish pull.

Example: “Only takes Mayfly” → choose Mayfly.
2

Choose how deep to fish

Press SPACE to start the depth meter. Press again to stop in Shallow, Midwater, or Deep. Deep is narrow; bigger, rarer fish live there.

3

Move the hook toward a fish

Steer with ← / → or the pond buttons. Move until the message says a fish is beside your lure.

4

Wait for a bite, then hook it

Wait for the fish to glow and show “Set the hook!” Then press SPACE. If it nibbles and leaves, line up again.

5

Reel the fish to the surface

Keep the marker in green to reel. Marker left of green: hold →. Marker right: hold ←.

Five casts. A miss uses one. New species raise your score.
