A little guide to the pond

How to play

  1. Choose a rod and bait to suit the fish you hope to find.
  2. Press SPACE or Cast to start the depth meter, then press again to stop. Deep water is a narrow target with rarer, bigger fish.
  3. As the pond scrolls and your lure sinks, steer with ← / → toward a fish. Get close and wait for it to bite.
  4. Press SPACE when the bite prompt appears to set the hook.
  5. During the fight, counter the fish with the opposite arrow. Keep tension in the green to reel it toward the surface.
